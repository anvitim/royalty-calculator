Liner notes

How the numbers work

Every number in the calculator carries a small tag.

FACT means the number comes straight from a source you can check, such as a government report or a company's price list. Tap the tag to open it.

ASSUMPTION means it's an estimate or a typical figure that varies from deal to deal. The tag explains why.

The rules are written and checked before anything is built. Read the full UK rules on GitHub and the India rules
