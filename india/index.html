The India calculator

Where the money from a stream goes in India, if you write and release your own non-film songs (songs that aren't made for films) through a distributor, in rupees.

The India figures are less certain than the UK ones. India publishes much less, so several numbers here, above all the value of one stream, are estimates. YouTube and short-video apps are left out, and that matters more in India than in the UK.
